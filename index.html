03 — Process

Five movements. Roughly. Order varies by project.

01
Concept
Read the brief twice, then forget it. Watch references with the sound off. Sketch the spine — what the cut is actually about.
Figma · Paper
02
Edit
Selects, structure, pacing. The rough cut is where the film either has a heartbeat or it doesn't. Most of the work happens here.
Premiere · Resolve
03
Motion
Type, transitions, graphics, comping. Add only what serves the cut. Subtract twice as much as you add.
After Effects · C4D
04
Sound
Score, design, foley accents. Sound is half the edit. Frames change weight when the audio finally locks.
Pro Tools · Reaper
05
Finish
Color, conform, deliverables. Aspect ratio cutdowns, captioning, master files. Boring on purpose.
Resolve · ffmpeg
After Effects Premiere Pro DaVinci Resolve Cinema 4D Redshift Figma Pro Tools Houdini · light
04 — About
Philippines · 03:14Frame 0001

I'm an editor first, and a motion designer second.

I've spent the last eight years cutting for agencies, studios, and a handful of bands who insisted on doing the whole thing in-camera. The work I'm proudest of is rarely the most decorated — it's the cut where the rhythm carries the idea without anybody noticing the seams.

I work best on projects with a strong director and a willingness to throw out the storyboard once the footage shows up. I'm calm in feedback, fast in turnaround, and quietly opinionated about pacing.
